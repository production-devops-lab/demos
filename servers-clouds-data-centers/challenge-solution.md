# Challenge solution: ten times more visitors, for one weekend

**The question:** your shop expects ten times more visitors, but only for one weekend. Do you buy more
servers (own) or rent them (cloud)? Why?

**A good answer: rent them, in the cloud.**

- You need the extra servers for **two days only**. Renting gives you more virtual machines **in
  minutes**, and you **give them back** on Monday, so you pay only while you use them.
- Buying means **waiting** for the delivery, placing the servers somewhere, and then owning machines that
  sit almost idle for the rest of the year.

**Honest note:** the cloud is not always cheaper. If you needed ten times more servers **every day, all
year**, owning them could cost less in the long run. The lesson's rule of thumb: rent for short or
changing needs; for steady needs, compare the costs.

**Remember:** rented or owned, a server is a server, in a real data center, in a real region. The cloud is
someone else's computers.
