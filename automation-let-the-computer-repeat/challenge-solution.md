# Challenge solution: which jobs to automate?

**The question:** which of these do you automate?

1. A report you build by hand every week, in 20 steps.
2. Moving your desk to another room.
3. A check, every minute, that the shop still answers.

**Use the three questions:** do I do it often? Is it the same steps every time? Does a mistake hurt?

| Job | Often? | Same steps? | Mistake hurts? | Automate? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly 20-step report | yes, every week | yes | yes, a wrong report | **yes** |
| Moving your desk | no, once | no | not much | **no** |
| Check every minute | yes, all day and night | yes | yes, nobody notices the shop is down | **yes** |

**Answer: automate 1 and 3.** Moving your desk happens once: writing automation for it would take longer
than doing it.

**About the check every minute:** no person can look at the shop every minute, all night. This is the
kind of job only a computer can do well. You will see these checks, and the alerts they send, in lesson 6.
